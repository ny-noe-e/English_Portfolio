Work & learning

Building things, one lesson at a time.

I work as a software engineering apprentice at Google in Zürich. My apprenticeship gives me the chance to learn in a real engineering environment, turn ideas into working software, and understand the care that goes into building technology people can depend on.

I enjoy solving problems, improving the details, and seeing a project move from an early thought to something useful. I’m still at the beginning of my career, which is exactly what makes it exciting: every project gives me something new to understand and a new way to improve.

Away from the screen

Focus, precision, discipline.

Outside work, one of my main hobbie is Swiss sport shooting with the SIG SG 550. I like the concentration it demands. Good results come from staying calm, handling the rifle responsibly, and repeating the fundamentals with patience.

That mindset carries into the rest of my life too. Whether I’m at the range or working through a difficult technical problem, I value precision, steady progress, and taking the time to do things properly.
